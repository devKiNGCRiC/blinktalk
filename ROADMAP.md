## 💡 Unique Feature Ideas

### 1. "Stranger Interests" Matching
- Users specify interests
- Match with strangers having similar interests
- Better than random matching

### 2. "Time Capsule" Messages
- Send messages to future
- Schedule messages
- Deliver on specific date/time

### 3. "Disappearing Messages"
- Messages auto-delete after time
- Privacy focused
- Like Snapchat

### 4. "Message in a Bottle"
- Send anonymous message to random user
- Fun and engaging
- Viral potential

### 5. "Chat Rooms by Interests"
- Auto-generated rooms by topics
- Programming, Movies, Sports, etc.
- AI-powered topic detection
